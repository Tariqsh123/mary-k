About the Author

Author of Shattered Roots to Stronger Wings, sharing a powerful journey of resilience, healing, and transformation. Through deeply personal experiences including childhood trauma, neglect, and domestic violence survival, she raises awareness about healing and recovery. She is also a community outreach medical advocate and prevention educator.
