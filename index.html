About the Author

Mary K Espinosa is a passionate Sexual Assault and Domestic Violence Medical Advocate, outreach leader, and prevention educator.

With a trauma-informed approach, she provides education and emotional support to teens, men, women, and children — focusing on awareness, empowerment, and healing. At the heart of her work is the belief that healing begins with being heard.

When asked by her son, “When are you going to retire?” her answer: “When there’s no more sexual assault or domestic violence.” His response? “Then never.”

Advocate Prevention Educator Storytelling for Healing
From Chapter 3 · Shattered Roots

“Looking back, I keep thinking that maybe my mom didn't care. The freedom we had felt like fun at the time, running around with nothing on our feet, but I now see a lack of attention. It was the feeling that no one was looking out for us.”

“Dad had converted an old bread truck into a camper. One day, Laura Mitch and I were hanging out, and Laura got ahold of a box of matches. I can still hear her voice, laughing as she lit a match, threw it on the bed and said to Mitch, ‘I'm going to teach you a lesson on how not to catch the truck on fire.’”

— raw memories, resilient voice
“There was a time when the weight of everything I'd been through felt too much to bear. The memories, the losses, and the pain that seemed to live inside me, they all came crashing down until there was nothing else in my mind but the thought of ending it all. I didn't think of my children, grandchildren, or my husband then. All I wanted was the pain inside me to stop.”
“It was terrifying to realize just how close I had come to letting go. The fact that I, who had endured so much and worked so hard to help others, could fall into such despair showed me the raw, isolating power of trauma. There's a depth to this kind of pain that people don't often talk about.”
— Shattered Roots to Stronger Wings

The Memoir

Shattered Roots to Stronger Wings

A courageous memoir of survival, healing, and reclaiming your voice. From childhood neglect to domestic violence, Mary K Espinosa shares her raw journey of breaking cycles and finding purpose. This is not just a story of pain — it's a testament to the unbreakable human spirit. Every page is a step from darkness into light.
