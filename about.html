About the Author

Mary K Espinosa is a passionate advocate, educator, and voice for healing.

She supports survivors through awareness, empowerment, and education — helping individuals find strength, voice, and recovery through a trauma-informed approach.

“Mary K Espinosa is a passionate Sexual Assault and Domestic Violence Medical Advocate, outreach leader, and prevention educator.”
“With a trauma-informed approach, she provides education and emotional support to teens, men, women, and children — focusing on awareness, empowerment, and healing. At the heart of her work is the belief that healing begins with being heard.”
“When asked by her son, ‘When are you going to retire?’ her answer: ‘When there’s no more sexual assault or domestic violence.’”
— His response: “Then never.”
